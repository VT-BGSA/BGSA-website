Graduate Student Resources

A curated starting point for Virginia Tech services that can support Biological Sciences graduate students throughout research, writing, professional development, funding, and graduate life.

Use the resources available to you

Virginia Tech provides a wide range of services for graduate students, many at no additional cost. The resources below are especially useful for developing research plans, analyzing data, improving manuscripts and proposals, preparing for careers, and navigating graduate school.

Start early

Writing, statistical, and research-support services are most useful when you contact them before a deadline or before analysis decisions become difficult to change.

Schedules, eligibility, and application deadlines may change. Always confirm current details on the official Virginia Tech resource page.

Build stronger research

These services can help at multiple stages of a project, from planning and analysis through writing, publication, and data stewardship.

Writing & Publishing

Virginia Tech Writing Center

The Virginia Tech Writing Center provides free writing support to Virginia Tech undergraduate and graduate students, faculty, and staff. Graduate students can use the Writing Center for course assignments as well as longer-term writing projects.

  • Brainstorming, organization, revision, and editing strategies
  • Manuscripts, proposals, theses, dissertations, and other projects
  • Face-to-face and live online writing consultations
  • Graduate-focused support for sustained writing projects
Writing Center Make an appointment
Located in the second-floor Learning Commons of Newman Library; online appointments are also available.
Statistics & Data Analysis

Statistical Applications and Innovations Group (SAIG)

SAIG, housed in the Department of Statistics, provides statistical advice, analysis, and education to Virginia Tech researchers. Support is available for both quick questions and more substantial research collaborations.

  • Experimental design and study or sampling plans
  • Statistical modeling, predictive analytics, and interpretation
  • Data visualization and statistical software or coding help
  • Grant proposals and publication preparation
Explore SAIG Project collaboration Drop-in consulting
Statistical assistance is free for Virginia Tech faculty, staff, and students on academic research projects; consult SAIG's current policies for service limits and availability.

Graduate School Professional Development

The Graduate School offers professional-development seminars, workshops, and courses designed to build skills useful during graduate school and beyond.

  • Professional-development seminars and workshops
  • Publication Academy courses
  • Career, mentoring, supervision, and time-management skills
  • Connections to additional university professional-development programs
Professional development
Conference Travel

GPSS Travel Fund Program

The Graduate and Professional Student Senate Travel Fund Program provides competitive grants that can partially offset eligible conference-travel expenses for graduate students presenting Virginia Tech research.

  • Support may include transportation, registration, lodging, and meals
  • Applications are organized into funding cycles
  • Eligibility and deadlines vary by cycle
  • Plan ahead and review the current application instructions carefully
Travel Fund Program
Funding is competitive and is not guaranteed. Check the current GPSS cycle before making travel plans.
Conflict & Graduate Support

Graduate School Ombudsperson

The Graduate School Ombuds Office provides an independent, impartial, informal, and confidential place to discuss concerns, conflicts, policies, and possible paths forward.

  • Discuss concerns involving advisors, colleagues, or university processes
  • Clarify policies, procedures, and available options
  • Explore communication and conflict-resolution strategies
  • Receive referrals to other appropriate campus resources when needed
